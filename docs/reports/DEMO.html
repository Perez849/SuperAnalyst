Catalizadores

What each lever is worth

  • Cost of capital: every 75bps of WACC moves fair value by ≈$25.80/share (11%). Rate path and the equity risk premium are the single biggest swing factor.
  • Terminal growth: 50bps of perpetuity growth is worth ≈$8.79/share — the market's read on the company's long-run competitive position.
  • Operating margin: each 100bps of EBIT margin across the forecast is worth ≈$8.45/share — watch pricing power, mix and cost discipline in quarterly prints.
  • Top-line delivery: our base assumes 7.0% year-one growth fading to 2.5%; sustained beats/misses versus that glide path are the cleanest re-rating trigger either way.
Riesgos

Key risks

  • Estimate risk: consensus forecasts can be revised sharply, and the forward-multiple lenses move one-for-one with the earnings they are applied to.
  • Multiple risk: target multiples are anchored on sector medians; a de-rating of the peer group lowers the target regardless of company performance.
  • Single third-party data source: restatements, non-recurring items or classification differences can shift the output.

Methodology & disclaimer. Generated by an automated, sector-aware valuation engine from public data (DEMO (synthetic)). DEMO was classified as Standard (forward multiples + DCF). The 12-month price target triangulates several lenses — target multiples on consensus forward earnings (peer/sector-anchored and adjusted for durable growth, leverage and earnings durability), a 10-year DCF with decaying growth and mean-reverting margins, and sector-specific models — weights them by profile, applies plausibility guardrails, rolls the result forward at the cost of equity net of dividends, and lightly anchors it to Street consensus when available. Reference multiples are long-run developed-market medians; live peer medians replace them when fetched.
