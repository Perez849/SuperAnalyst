Catalizadores

What each lever is worth

  • Cost of capital: every 75bps of WACC moves fair value by ≈$0.04/share (-13%). Rate path and the equity risk premium are the single biggest swing factor.
  • Terminal growth: 50bps of perpetuity growth is worth ≈$0.00/share — the market's read on the company's long-run competitive position.
  • Operating margin: each 100bps of EBIT margin across the forecast is worth ≈$0.03/share — watch pricing power, mix and cost discipline in quarterly prints.
  • Top-line delivery: our base assumes 25.0% year-one growth fading to 2.5%; sustained beats/misses versus that glide path are the cleanest re-rating trigger either way.
Riesgos

Key risks

  • Through-cycle margin and multiple are estimates; a structurally lower commodity price or cost inflation would reset the normalised earnings base.
  • Low-confidence valuation: the lenses disagree or the inputs carry data-quality flags. Treat the target as indicative.
  • Estimate risk: consensus forecasts can be revised sharply, and the forward-multiple lenses move one-for-one with the earnings they are applied to.
  • Multiple risk: target multiples are anchored on sector medians; a de-rating of the peer group lowers the target regardless of company performance.
  • Single third-party data source: restatements, non-recurring items or classification differences can shift the output.
