11Catalysts & what each lever is worth

  • Cost of capital: every 75bps of WACC moves fair value by ≈$32.85/share (7%). Rate path and the equity risk premium are the single biggest swing factor.
  • Terminal growth: 50bps of perpetuity growth is worth ≈$0.79/share — the market's read on the company's long-run competitive position.
  • Operating margin: each 100bps of EBIT margin across the forecast is worth ≈$34.19/share — watch pricing power, mix and cost discipline in quarterly prints.
  • Top-line delivery: our base assumes 168.8% year-one growth fading to 2.5%; sustained beats/misses versus that glide path are the cleanest re-rating trigger either way.

12Key risks

  • Earnings are cyclically elevated: forward EPS is 20.0x the through-cycle average. A normalisation of pricing, volumes or margins would reset the earnings base the multiples are applied to.
  • Our model-only target is -38% versus the Street consensus ($1,975.95); where the model is right the stock has an information edge, where it is wrong the Street's inputs deserve more weight.
  • Estimate risk: consensus forecasts can be revised sharply, and the forward-multiple lenses move one-for-one with the earnings they are applied to.
  • Multiple risk: target multiples are anchored on sector medians; a de-rating of the peer group lowers the target regardless of company performance.
  • Single third-party data source: restatements, non-recurring items or classification differences can shift the output.
