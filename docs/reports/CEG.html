Appendix

Method and definitions

  1. Lenses and weightsThe target triangulates relative lenses (target multiples on consensus forward numbers, anchored on peers/sector references and adjusted for durable growth, leverage and earnings durability), a 10-year DCF (consensus for the near years, geometric growth decay, mean-reverting margins) and sector models, weighted by company profile.
  2. GuardrailsA lens outside 0.2×–5× the price is excluded; lenses > 2.2× away from the median of the others are down-weighted by 60%; the published target is limited to 0.4×–2.5× the price; fewer than two usable lenses, or a result forced by a guardrail, is published NOT RATED.
  3. 12-month targetFair value × (1 + cost of equity) − dividends; bear/bull from the lens ranges; Street consensus anchored at up to 30% (scaled by analyst count) and always shown separately.
  4. Scorecard scalesValuation: expected return −30%…+50% → 0…100. Growth: durable EPS growth 0…25% and FY+1 revenue −5…+30%. Profitability: ROIC−WACC −5…+25 pts and EBIT margin 0…35%. Quality: Piotroski share of tests passed, OCF/NI 0.5…1.2, accruals. Balance sheet: net debt/EBITDA, interest cover, Altman zone. Cycle fit: Reloj tilt −4…+4 pp/yr.
  5. Piotroski · Altman · SloanPiotroski F: nine binary tests of profitability, leverage/liquidity and efficiency (tests that cannot be computed from the available statement lines are excluded, not failed). Altman Z (original industrial form): >2.99 safe, 1.81–2.99 grey, <1.81 distress. Sloan accruals: (net income − OCF) / average assets.
  6. Reloj de inversiónPhase probabilities and sector evidence come from the linked project's published data (see source above). Tilt = Σ phase probability × shrunk excess return of the sector in that phase. It describes historical behaviour and is not an input to the price target.
